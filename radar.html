Weather & Radar Layer

This live map shows current weather patterns and radar echoes. During peak migration seasons, some of the nighttime radar “clutter” in clear skies can actually be birds moving at altitude.

How this relates to bird migration

Bird migration is tightly linked to weather: winds, fronts, and clear skies can all help or hinder birds on the move. By watching radar and weather layers together, you can start to see when conditions are right for big migration nights.

Future versions of MigrationWatch can layer species data, migration forecasts, and seasonal summaries on top of views like this.
