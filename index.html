Featured Migratory Birds

Four iconic migrants that help tell the story of North American migration. Hover over each card to get a closer look.

American Robin
Across North America
A common backyard bird whose red-orange breast and early dawn songs signal spring.
Snow Goose
Arctic ⇄ Southern US
Massive white flocks migrate in spectacular waves across the continent every fall and spring.
Sandhill Crane
Great Plains ⇄ Gulf Coast & Mexico
Known for their bugling calls and huge migratory gatherings at key wetlands.
Canada Goose
Canada ⇄ USA
Famous for V-shaped flights and loud honks—one of the most recognized migrants.
