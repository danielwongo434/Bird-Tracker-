Featured North American Migrants

A few iconic species that help tell the story of migration. Images are placeholders; future versions can link each card to live maps and seasonal status.

Canada Goose
Arctic & Canada ⇄ United States
Famous V-formations and loud calls. Many populations now winter in temperate cities and migrate shorter distances than they used to.
Sandhill Crane
Great Plains ⇄ Mexico & Gulf Coast
Massive flocks funnel through key stopover sites. Their bugling calls and circling kettles make migration visible and loud.
American Robin
Across North America
A familiar backyard bird whose arrival and dawn song signal spring for much of the continent.
Snow Goose
High Arctic ⇄ Southern US & Mexico
Enormous white flocks that turn marshes and fields into moving clouds during peak migration.

Where this project is going

This is version 1: a static front page with animated text and sample species. Next steps include embedding live migration maps, pulling data from public APIs, and building a small database that tracks seasonal status by region.
